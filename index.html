Agents don't get adopted because they're smart. They get adopted because someone decided to trust one.

Capability is commoditizing fast. The winners won't be whoever has the smartest model. They'll be whoever makes agents legible, controllable, and continuously verified. That layer, between "the agent works" and "the org relies on it," is where the durable company gets built.

The product

One teammate per team. One place to see them all.

Each team shapes its own agent, one that knows how they work, plugs into the tools they already use, and takes the routine work off their plate. You get a live, legible view of everything it does, and a control surface for everything that matters.

Live status on what each agent is doing right now, and why. The work is the interface: everything it does is attributable, reviewable, and yours to open at any time.

Control

Autonomy you can switch off, gate, and bound.

How it works

Onboard a team in a day.

Not a rollout project. A persona, its access, and a first task, then it compounds.

Grant access
